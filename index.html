I am an Associate in Statistics and Data Science student at PolyU Hong Kong Community College (PolyU HKCC) and a collaborating student researcher with the Machine Learning & Optimization Group (CityU-MLO) at City University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Chen Liu.

My research interests lie in tool-using large language model (LLM) agents, quantitative AI & formulaic alpha-factor mining, reproducible benchmarking, and computer vision — including co-first-authored work on QFinZero (ACL 2026 System Demonstrations) and co-authored work on AlphaBench (ICLR 2026) and EvoAlpha (NeurIPS 2025 Workshop).

Beyond academic research, I compete in machine learning challenges on Kaggle (Silver Medalist) and publish technical notes on deep learning, generative AI, and cognitive science on my BlackCat Blog, Zhihu, and CSDN.
